About Annai Velankanni School

Established in 2004 with a mission to deliver transformative, values-based English medium education in Pavoorchatram, Tenkasi District.

Two Decades of Distinction (2004 – Present)

A Journey of Faith, Knowledge & Character

In 2004, Annai Velankanni Matriculation Higher Secondary School was founded in Sivagamipuram, Pavoorchatram. Built with a vision to provide world-class English medium education within reach of families in the Tenkasi and Tirunelveli rural regions, the institution has grown into an acclaimed center of scholastic rigor and moral nurturing.

From our iconic 4-storey azure campus to our extensive playgrounds and advanced labs, every facet of our campus is designed to unlock each child's potential. Recognized under the Tamil Nadu Matriculation Board (UDISE: 33290704117), our alumni have secured seats in premier medical colleges, top engineering institutes, chartered accountancy firms, and civil services.

Spiritual Patroness & Mountain Vista

Guided by Our Lady of Good Health

Perched atop our campus entrance overlooking the lush rural greenery of Sivagamipuram stands the golden shrine of Our Lady of Good Health (Annai Velankanni). Behind the shrine rise the majestic, mist-kissed ridges of the Western Ghats mountain range of Tenkasi.

This serene spiritual presence serves as a daily blessing for students of all faiths and backgrounds, instilling values of peace, maternal compassion, selfless service, and pursuit of wisdom.
