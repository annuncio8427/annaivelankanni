Dedicated Physics, Chemistry & Biology Labs

Equipped to state board matriculation standards, our science laboratories enable each student to perform individual experiments under the vigilant guidance of qualified demonstrators.

Physics: Optical benches, galvanometers, resistance boxes, potentiometer, sonometer, and vernier instruments.

Biology: Compound microscopes, permanent biological slides, preserved specimen jars, and anatomical models.
