Handwriting & Reading Syllabus (Rule 15)

Handwriting and daily reading in every subject is an integrated, compulsory component of the curriculum from Std I to Std X:

  • Curricular Integration: Graded penmanship and reading assignments across all subjects.
  • Scheduled Submissions: Regular portfolio evaluations to ensure neat cursive script and articulate speech.
  • English-Only Campus (Rule 3): Daily immersion in spoken English across classrooms and campus grounds.
Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

English is the primary medium of instruction across all grades from Pre-KG to Class XII. In line with Rule 3 of our Student Handbook, only English should be spoken within the school premises to foster natural fluency. Tamil is taught with high literary rigor as the first/second language in full accordance with Tamil Nadu state board regulations.
Annai Velankanni Matriculation Higher Secondary School is recognized by the Government of Tamil Nadu under the Directorate of Matriculation Schools (UDISE Code: 33290704117) for both 10th SSLC and 12th Higher Secondary Board examinations.
We conduct special evening remedial coaching classes, personalized one-on-one doubt clearing sessions with subject teachers, structured handwriting and reading evaluation portfolios (Std I to Std X), and periodic parent-teacher meetings to ensure every child makes consistent progress.
Under Rule 5 of our Student Handbook, every student must compulsorily take part in any one of four disciplines: Yoga, Skating, Silambam, or Karate, in addition to regular school games (basketball, volleyball, athletics). We also host science exhibitions, elocution, quiz competitions, and cultural celebrations featured on our official YouTube channel AVM STARS.
