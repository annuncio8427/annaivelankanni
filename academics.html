English is the primary medium of instruction across all grades from Pre-KG to Class XII. Tamil is taught with high literary rigor as the first/second language in full accordance with Tamil Nadu state board regulations.
Annai Velankanni Matriculation Higher Secondary School is recognized by the Government of Tamil Nadu under the Directorate of Matriculation Schools (UDISE Code: 33290704117) for both 10th SSLC and 12th Higher Secondary Board examinations.
