Official Student Diary & Handbook

Norms of Rules & Discipline

"The school expects of every student to contribute towards the discipline and moral character of the school."

Official statutory norms binding all enrolled students and parents at Annai Velankanni Matriculation Higher Secondary School.

School Timings

9:00 AM – 4:00 PM daily. All students must be inside campus before 8:50 AM.

English Medium Campus

Only English should be spoken within school premises to foster fluency and communication skills.

The 3 Golden Phrases

Pupils must excel in courtesy, never forgetting: PLEASE, THANK YOU, and SORRY.

White Ribbon Hair Code

Std IV to XII girls with hair below shoulders must plait, fold, and secure with a white ribbon.

Mandatory Activity

Compulsory choice of 1 activity: Yoga, Skating, Silambam, or Karate.

Handwriting & Reading

Handwriting & reading in every subject is part of the curriculum from Std I to Std X.

Zero tolerance for bullying, bad language, property damage, or indiscipline. Strictly Enforced
Zero tolerance for bullying, bad language, property damage, or indiscipline. Strictly Enforced
1 Campus Timings & Punctuality
  • R1. Daily Hours: School begins punctually at 9:00 AM and closes at 4:00 PM.
  • R2. Morning Arrival: Students must be present inside the school premises before 8:50 AM for morning assemblies.
  • R4. Late Coming Policy: Habitual late coming will result in formal disciplinary dismissal.
Punctuality is the foundation of character
2 English Campus & Courtesy Code
  • R3. English Medium: Only English should be spoken within the school premises at all times.
  • R6. Golden Manners: Pupils must be gentle and courteous, never forgetting the golden phrases "PLEASE", "THANK YOU", and "SORRY".
  • R6b. Zero Bullying: Bad language, bullying, and disrespectful behaviour will not be tolerated. Habitual offenders face suspension or expulsion.
  • R12. Classroom Decorum: Strict silence must be observed in classes whether the teacher is present or not.
Cultivating noble character & respectful speech
3 Compulsory Sports & Martial Arts
  • R5. Universal Participation: Every student must take part in school games and activities unless medically certified unfit or exempted by the Principal.
  • R5b. Compulsory Activity Selection: Students must compulsorily take part in any one of:
    Yoga Skating Silambam Karate
Physical vigor & traditional martial arts mastery
4 Sacred Uniform & House Uniform
  • R7. Sacred Possession: School uniform is a sacred possession cultivating Mutual Respect, Equality, and Confidence.
  • R7b. Off-Campus Restriction: Visiting restaurants, cinema multiplexes, or public places in school uniform is a serious breach of discipline inviting firm action.
  • R8. Wednesday House Uniform: On Wednesdays, students must come in their designated House Uniform.
  • R9. Strict Conformity: The pattern of the uniform must be followed very strictly at all times.
Equality & collective identity through uniform sanctity
5 Personal Grooming & Footwear
  • R11. Hair Code (Std IV to XII): Girls with hair below shoulders must plait it, fold it, and secure the plait with a white ribbon. Hair must be neatly groomed.
  • R10. Rainy Season Footwear: On rainy days, students are permitted to use only Black Sandals. Wearing slippers to school is strictly forbidden.
  • R8b/R11b. Strictly Forbidden: Fancy/costly ornaments, makeup, eye makeup, nail polish, applying mehandi, fancy hair clips, fancy earrings, hair coloring, inappropriate hair styling, and body tattoos.
Simplicity, modesty & immaculate personal hygiene
6 Academics, Property & Parent Code
  • R15. Handwriting & Reading Syllabus: Handwriting and reading in every subject is part of the curriculum from Std I to Std X. Assignments must be submitted on specified dates for evaluation.
  • R14. School Diary Declaration: Parents are requested to go through the details given in the school diary and sign in the declaration.
  • R13. Grievance Redressal: Parents are requested to make complaints directly to school authorities (Principal / Office) and not to class teachers.
  • R16. Zero Vandalism: No scratching or writing on desks, walls, or furniture. Accidental damage must be reported immediately; damages will be made good by the person responsible. Anyone seeing damage must report it.
Parent-school partnership & civic responsibility
Student & Parent Undertaking

All rules in the school diary are to be read and signed during admission enrollment.
